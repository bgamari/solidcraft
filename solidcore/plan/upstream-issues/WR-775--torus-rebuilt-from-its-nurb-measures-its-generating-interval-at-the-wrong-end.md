#### WR-775. `SmTorus::RebuildSTEPFromNURBParameters` measures the generating interval's end at the sweep's far end, where its generator does not
- **Severity:** Wrong result (the rebuilt torus fails `SmTorus` Test #2)
- **Area:** Geometry
- **Location:** `source/SMLib/src/SmTorus.cpp`, `SmTorus::RebuildSTEPFromNURBParameters` (and `IsNurbSurfaceTorus`, which builds its interval the same way); the generator's interval is re-measured in `source/SMLib/src/SmEllipse.cpp`, `SmPolarConversion::SetUpPolarConversion`.
- **What happens:** the torus's generating (V) interval runs from the angle of the NURB's first corner, measured about a centre at the sweep's start, to the angle of its last corner, measured about a centre at the sweep's end. Both centres are placed with the fitted major radius. The generating circle is then built at the sweep's start, and `SetUpPolarConversion` measures its own ends from the NURB's first iso curve about that circle's centre, snapping them to the interval it was given only within `1000 * SM_EFF_ZERO` degrees. A tube end at right angles to the radial direction (90 or 270 degrees) turns a major radius error of δ into an angle error of δ / r radians at the far end, which the iso curve at the start does not share. With δ a few 1e-11 on a minor radius of about 1, the two ends differ by more than 1e-9 degrees, past the snap and past `HasSameParameterization`'s `SM_EFF_ZERO * (1 + 270)`, so Test #2 ("Same Parameterization") refuses the torus.
- **Trigger:** a torus R 70.168, r 1.089, V 150 to 270 degrees, sweep 0 to 102 degrees, read back from its NURB form with about 1e-13 of rounding in the control points. The NURB differs from the exact one by 1.1e-13, the fitted R by 2.7e-11, and the rebuilt interval ends at 270.0000000012252 against the generator's 270.000000000006. In SolidCraft, a body split once and read back from its record refused every further operation (an in-app report of a Split Body on a revolved part).
- **How found:** tracing that report: the piece written by the first split failed the full check right after `Brep::from_record`.
- **Confirmed:** on the port at `182d2c6bd`, which follows the C++. Solidcore now takes the torus's generating interval from the generator it has just built (`surface::torus`, test `rebuild_from_a_rounded_nurb_keeps_the_generator_interval`, which fails without the change). Within the snap the two are the same value, so tori that passed before are unchanged.
